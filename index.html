MEET KOUSHIK

The trader behind the charts.

Full Time Trader 📈📉I’m NISM Certified Reg No-202500055982

Koushik approaches the market as a probability game, not a prediction contest. His work centers on reading price action and market structure, identifying where liquidity sits, and only acting when several independent signals line up. Position sizing and stop placement are decided before an idea is ever considered a trade.

Behind the charts is a study in temperament as much as technique — recognising when a setup is genuinely there, and when the pull to act is just impatience wearing a disguise. Consistency, to Koushik, is built one well-managed loss at a time.

THE FRAMEWORK

The Koushik Framework

A hypothetical, repeatable process for evaluating and managing trades — built to remove guesswork, not add mystique.

Market Structure

Identify trends, breakouts, support, resistance, liquidity zones, and key levels.

Confluence

Combine price action, structure, momentum, and higher-timeframe context.

Entry

Wait for confirmation instead of entering purely from prediction.

Risk Management

Use predefined stop-loss levels and controlled position sizing.

Exit

Use risk/reward targets and market structure to manage exits.
